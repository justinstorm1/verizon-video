The obvious question

If everyone gets a slice, isn’t it just normal 5G?

Every campus cell
35%Guarantee cap
65%Always kept for regular users
  1. Guarantees are capped

    No more than 35% of any cell. Regular users always keep the other 65%.

  2. Seats and hours are limited

    When a plan is full on a cell, new sign-ups join a waitlist.

  3. No silent slowdowns

    A boost that won’t fit is refused with the next open time. It never starts slow.

Priority 5G 06
How it works

From subscription to a guaranteed boost.

01Plan & boost

Subscribe online. Tap to start a boost.

02Admission control

Sells seats only while they fit under the cap.

03Verizon 5G SA core

Enforces each plan’s slice and priority.

04SLA monitoring

Checks every boost. Refunds any hour that misses.

Monitoring feeds back into admission control to tune seat limits.
Production path: 3GPP NEF or CAMARA-style Quality-on-Demand APIs. Monitoring uses aggregate network data only.
